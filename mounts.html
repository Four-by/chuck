Continuing the installation of a 300 Ford six into my 1959 F100...

This is the pattern for the left side mount. It was necessary to make the pattern in two parts to be able to print them out. If you save the images to your computer, and print them with no scaling, they should be the proper size for your template. Print the upper and lower half, then simply join them together with a clear tape. Be sure to check for accuracy, the squares in the background are 1/4", also measure the 8 1/8" distance to see that it is close.
