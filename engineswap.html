The Install




Well, approximately 2 weeks ago I started converting my ’59 F100 short bed from a 223 6cyl 4 spd, to a 300 6cyl with a c4 auto. I had previously found a 1978 F150 that had a rebuilt 300 six, and C4 auto tranny.

In my younger days I worked as a mechanic, and have been involved in mechanics and body work as a hobby most of my life, however I am now 60+ yrs. of age and not in the best of health. So this was probably my last hurrah so to speak.

I pre-fabricated my front mounts, and ordered the rear C4 tranny mount. I also replaced most gaskets and seals on both the engine and transmission, installed EFI exhaust manifolds, and a cheap chrome valve cover.

Tuesday morning I drained the old 223 of most fluids, and the next thing you know I was well on my way to wrenching everything loose. I was surprised how quickly this old man was able to get things done once I got started. By noon, engine and transmission were out and most remaining fluids that spilled out were cleaned up. ( Doing this project in our attached 2 stall garage, wife's vehicle outside, and “stinking up the garage”)

I rested the next day… well actually cleaned up a few things in the engine compartment, set the C4 temporarily in place through the engine compartment ( so I would not have to do it later from underneath), and prayed my pre-fabbed front mounts were going to fit.

The next morning after having my 4 AM morning coffee and BS session at the local bakery, I decided it was time to set the new power plant in place ( BTW I had the use of a good “cherry picker” from a friend of mine, but all the work was done solo).

Heck just lifting the engine and having it momentarily hang over my recently acquired new grille and bumper was scary… ( never used to give it a second thought… damned old age!!) Anyway it was over in about 15 minutes… engine was in, flush against the tranny ( which was still suspended with the floor jack since I did not know the exact location of the tranny mount at this point.) and my pre-fabbed mounts fit perfectly! WOO HOOO!!!

Then I rested, and breathed a sigh of relief…. Later that day I installed the tranny mount, and connected the other items that could be connected with out any major modification.. i.e. trans cooler lines ( I simply used the ones from the donor truck ), oil pressure, temp gauge, heater hoses, etc. .

Friday, my goal was to install the radiator and since I had not decided on which one I was going to use ( donor truck rad or my original one.. which must have been previously replaced since it had the built in oil cooler, but the fittings would not line up with the previously saved lines ), I opted for the donor one, and adapted it by merely adding a 1/8” by 2” by about 18” length of flat iron to the outer edge and bolting it in. I thought I would need a shorter water pump, but had no problem using the stock 300 water pump, fan and 1 inch fan spacer( actually I have about 2 ¼” inches between the fan and the radiator ).

My plan was to do the electrical last, so I was still sticking to my plan, although getting anxious to temporarily FIRE IT UP! ( the years have made me older, but still not much for patience! )

I still needed to do a few miscellaneous things, fabricate a drive shaft, and wire it before start up.

Saturday morning.. ( again after the trip to the bakery ), I got started on things.. next thing I know I’m searching for a friend of mine to weld up my newly fabricated drive shaft.. ( quite easily adapted donor truck shaft with my ’59 rear joint assembly sawed off my old drive shaft. Shaft pipes were the same size.) I can weld quite good.. problem is.. I don’t see good enough to follow the seam! ( damned old age!! ) Anyway a bit later I am back home with driveshaft installed, temporarily hot wired, and pouring in some new fluids. ( Really anxious now!!

I jump in… ( well not really jump), actually crawled in and fired it up: no gas in carb yet so it turned over a few times while I was hoping my wiring was correct. .. SUDDENLY it fired!! right out of the header pipes ( no exhaust system yet ). YIPPEE!!!

Then came the downer, no leaks anywhere…. But as I was getting the tranny filled up I noticed some of my tranny fluid was dripping out the front seal…OH ****!!! I said.
Yup I said that!

Okay okay I will shorten this up..geesh

I pulled the tranny, checked the seal... it was fine.

As it turns out the converter was accidently dropped, it looked fine but must have been tweaked enough to make it out of round.

I ordered a new converter, installed a new seal, reinstalled the tranny and it is fine now... Phew, glad that is done! Hard for an old guy like me to be crawling around underneath.

I have since installed my Lokar shifter, new carpet, and just now finishing up the detailing.... BUT I love it!!!!!

If anyone wants or needs any details or specifics, I will be happy to share those with you.

Had the new dual glass packs with an H-pipe installed yesterday...mmmm.. sounds nice!
