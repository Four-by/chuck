When I purchased my '59 in late June of 2008, it was in pretty good condition... but of course everyone has their own definition. In my case, I like to detail things and inspect items so I know for myself what condition they are in. I bought it from a gentleman near Minneapolis, and drove it back home to west central Minnesota... It had a 223 6cyl, and a 4spd tranny, both had been recently overhauled. The rear axle ratio had been changed to taller gears, but even with the 3.54:1 ratio, 60 mph is really revving the 223

My original plan was to locate a 3spd overdrive tranny for it and replace the granny geared 4spd...hmmmm.. easier said than done, there are a few overdrive tranny's out there from cars, but since the input shaft is different I did not want to mess with locating various parts.

I started thinking about other options, I really did not want to go with a V-8 auto..

As it turned out, a friend knew where there was a 1978 Pickup with a low mileage 300 six and C-4 automatic in it (Pictured here). I had not considered this as a likely conversion until that time... Hmm.. the more I thought about it , I liked that idea ( plus the price was very reasonable )!
